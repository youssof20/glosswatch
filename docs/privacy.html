Glosswatch

Privacy policy

Publisher: youssof20 · Updated September 13, 2026

Glosswatch helps you learn words from your videos and subtitles. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or backend. The extension does not send your videos, subtitles, browsing activity, settings, or saved words to the publisher or to a remote service.

What the extension accesses

On supported pages, Glosswatch locates video elements and reads their playback time and position to display your subtitles. A video identifier is hashed locally to remember timing adjustments. It does not build or transmit a browsing-history record.

The local player reads video and subtitle files you explicitly choose or drop. Files are not uploaded. Supported MKV files are copied into a temporary MP4 in the browser's private storage without re-encoding.

What stays on your device

Extension storage holds preferences and timing offsets. A local database holds saved words, meanings, subtitle sentences, translations, source filenames, and review schedules. These records remain until you remove them or uninstall the extension. They are not synchronized between devices.

Temporary video files are removed when you change videos or leave the local player. Files left after an interruption are cleaned up the next time a local player opens. You can export your saved words and review schedule as a JSON backup, or words as a text file for Anki. Exported files remain wherever you save them, even after uninstalling.

Dictionaries and pronunciation

All five dictionaries ship with the extension. Word lookup does not contact a server. Pronunciation is offered only when a local system voice is available.

External links

Opening a dictionary source link takes you to Wiktionary. Support email opens your chosen email application. Those services handle your interaction under their own policies. Glosswatch does not automatically send them your local files or collection.

Permissions

Page access allows the subtitle layer to find videos, including embedded players. Active-tab access lets the toolbar act on the page you are using and explain restricted-page or file-access limitations. Storage access keeps preferences and saved words on your device.

Your choices

You can remove individual saved words, edit meanings, export a backup, or uninstall Glosswatch to remove its local extension data. You can also restrict the extension's site access using your browser's extension settings.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact youssof20 at chuumberry@gmail.com. If you email support, the publisher receives the address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and uses them to respond to your request. This is separate from the extension, which does not upload data.
