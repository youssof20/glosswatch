Glosswatch

A few useful details.

On a website

Start the video and click the Glosswatch toolbar icon, or the Glosswatch button on the video. Choose your subtitle file. If subtitles are early, increase the timing offset; if they’re late, decrease it. Timing is remembered for each video and subtitle file.

Use the panel’s Full screen button to keep subtitles visible. Some players enter native video-only full screen, which cannot display an extension overlay. Exit that mode and use Glosswatch’s button instead. Browser pages, closed shadow roots, and players without an accessible video element cannot be overlaid.

Local files

The local player opens files directly from your device. MKV files with supported codecs are copied into a temporary MP4 in browser storage, without re-encoding. Preparing a file needs roughly its size in free browser storage. The temporary file is removed when you change videos or leave the player. Unsupported codecs such as some HEVC or surround-audio variants need a different version of the video.

To use a video in a file:// tab in Chrome, enable “Allow access to file URLs” in Glosswatch’s extension details. The local player does not need that permission.

Subtitles & words

SRT, VTT, ASS and SSA are supported. ASS styling is reduced to plain text. Image subtitles (.sub/.idx) are not supported. If text looks wrong, choose its encoding under Appearance & text. Translation subtitles use timing overlap; “No matching translation” means the files don’t overlap confidently at that moment. Each track has its own offset.

Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese word meanings are bundled offline. Definitions are vocabulary aids, not sentence translations. Some words may be absent or ambiguous. You can enter your own meaning for missing words and edit saved meanings. Listen is available when the browser has an offline voice for the selected language.

Your data

Glosswatch has no accounts, analytics, ads or backend. Videos and subtitles stay on this device. Settings are stored in extension storage; saved words and review schedules are stored in IndexedDB. Nothing is synced to another device. Back up your saved words before uninstalling. Clicking a Wiktionary source link opens that website; lookups themselves use only the bundled dictionary.

The extension runs content scripts on web pages to locate videos. Active-tab access lets the toolbar open the panel or explain why a page can’t be used. Storage access saves preferences and words. No page content is sent to a server.
